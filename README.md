Join the Student Life is a community platform that helps international and exchange students connect before they arrive at their destination. By offering city‑specific groups, events, and a social network, it enables newcomers to find friends, get local tips, and feel at home quickly. The service spans over 85 cities worldwide, supporting students from 500+ universities with resources, mentorship, and a vibrant online community.

**Areas:** Community · Education · International · Students · Travel

## How this profile is made

API Evangelist profiles a company's public API surface the way a developer would find it: the website, developer portal and documentation, the specifications it publishes, its public repositories, and its pricing, status and changelog pages. From that it derives the properties above — contracts split per API, workflows, schemas, collections, governance rules, plans and rate limits — and scores them against the published [Kin Score rubric](https://github.com/api-evangelist/kin-score).

The artifacts are published on [APIs.io](https://apis.io), not in this repository. This repository is the public record for Join the Student Life: the place to say something is wrong, missing or out of date, and to ask for a re-score or a removal.
